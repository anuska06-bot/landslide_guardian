🛡️ Citizen Action Advisory Protocols NDMA Guidelines
🟡 Moderate Alert Phase
  • Inspect slope retaining walls and drains for clogging.
  • Look for new ground fissures or door/window sticking.
  • Keep battery bank and emergency radio charged.
🟠 High Risk Protocol
  • Avoid mountain highway travel unless emergency authorized.
  • Sleep in rooms furthest from the upslope hill face.
  • Pack Grab-and-Go kit (medicines, documents, torch).
🔴 Critical / SOS Trigger
  • IMMEDIATE EVACUATION: Move to designated shelters.
  • Listen for loud rumbling sounds or cracking trees.
  • Dial District Emergency Operations Center (1077 / 112).
